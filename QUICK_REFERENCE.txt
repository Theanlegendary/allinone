💡 WHY USE /morning?
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  Problem:  Package held 15 hours (most overnight when shop closed)
            Regular report shows 🔴 RED → Looks urgent
  
  Solution: /morning subtracts 12h overnight → Shows 3h actual work time
            Morning report shows 🟢 GREEN → Fair assessment
  
  Result:   Manager focuses on truly urgent items (>10h actionable)
            Not penalized for unavoidable overnight hold
