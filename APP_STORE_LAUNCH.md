Welcome to Sanctuary — your personal space for instant peace, soft focus, and deep, restorative sleep.

Whether you are looking to wash away evening stress, drift into cozy slumber, or create a quiet background environment for deep work, Sanctuary offers an unrestricted multi-track ambient soundscape experience.

✨ WHY YOU WILL LOVE SANCTUARY:

🎛️ 78 MULTI-LAYER AMBIENT SOUNDS
Mix and match up to 78 high-definition CC0 soundscapes across 8 categories:
- Rain: Gentle Downpour, Thunderstorm, Rain on Roof, Window Drops
- Nature: Ocean Waves, Forest Birds, Campfire, Mountain Stream
- Places: Cozy Coffee Shop, Library, Zen Garden, Temple Bowls
- Noise & Frequencies: Solfeggio 432Hz, Brown Noise, Pink Noise

🌸 ULTRA-SOFT STRESS RELIEF DESIGN
Built with gentle copy, warm colors, and calming typography specifically crafted to reduce anxiety and create an effortless, pressure-free sanctuary.

❤️ 1-TAP FAVORITES & CUSTOM MIXES
Save your top favorite soundscapes with a single tap. Rename custom ambient presets and enjoy seamless offline audio persistence.

🌙 SLEEP AUTO-FADE TIMER
Set auto-fade timers from 15 to 90 minutes. Soundscapes gently fade to silence as you drift into uninterrupted sleep.

⚡ FIRST-PARTY APPLE-LEVEL PERFORMANCE
Designed for silky-smooth 120 FPS ProMotion displays with instant startup and zero lag.
