Automated Dispatch Architecture

An executive study on real-time order telemetry, 4-stage operational partitioning, and multi-shift clearance monitoring across 38 nationwide post offices.

"Logistical velocity depends on decision clarity. By transforming raw Metfone TMS server feeds into structured executive telemetry, nationwide clearance times are reduced by 40%."

STAGE 01

អីវ៉ាន់ត្រូវយក (Pickup)

Tracks orders created and scheduled for collection at receiving stores across all 5 operational zones.

STAGE 02

អីវ៉ាន់ត្រូវចែក (Delivery)

Monitors active delivery dispatches assigned to couriers for final recipient drop-off.

STAGE 03

អីវ៉ាន់ផ្ញើបន្ត (Transit)

Manages inter-hub transfers, Send Mega dispatches, and sorting facility movement logs.

STAGE 04

អីវ៉ាន់មិនទាន់ (Branch)

Identifies pending unassigned inventory sitting at branch post offices to prevent bottleneck delays.
