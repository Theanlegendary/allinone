Why orders get pending in Branch (Not Assign) & Delivery tables, and how to resolve them by transitioning status 306 → 402 (10h KPI).

306
At Store / Accept Handover: Order pending at branch awaiting courier assignment.
402
Assignment Confirmed (Set KPI 10h): Courier assigned — 10h countdown starts!
410 / 420 / 430 / 460
Outcomes: 410 (Shipped) | 420 (Ship again) | 430 (Ship failed) | 460 (Return)
