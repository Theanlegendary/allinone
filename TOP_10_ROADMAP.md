# 🏆 Top 10 App Store Launch & Growth Roadmap

---

## 📅 Phase 1: Pre-Launch Setup & App Store Assets (Days 1–5)

1. **App Store Connect Setup**:
   - Register App ID (`com.sanctuary.relax`) and create App Store Connect listing.
   - Upload build via Xcode or Flutter CLI (`flutter build ipa`).

2. **App Store Visual Assets**:
   - Screenshot 1: *78-Track Ambient Mixer*
   - Screenshot 2: *Ultra-Soft Guided Meditation Cards*
   - Screenshot 3: *1-Tap Favorites & Custom Presets*
   - Screenshot 4: *Sleep Auto-Fade Timer*

---

## 🚀 Phase 2: Launch Week & Public Launch (Days 6–12)

1. **Product Hunt Launch**:
   - Post maker comment from `PRODUCT_HUNT_LAUNCH.md`.
   - Engage with voters and answer questions in real time.

2. **Apple Editorial Submission**:
   - Submit pitch from `APP_STORE_LAUNCH.md` via Apple's official Feature Pitch form.

3. **Viral Short-Form Content**:
   - Post 15-second ASMR videos showcasing custom sound mixes (*Rain + Coffee Shop + Fireplace*).

---

## 📈 Phase 3: Retention & Ratings Engine (Days 13–30)

1. **In-App Review Prompt**:
   - Prompt happy users for 5-star ratings after 3 completed sessions.

2. **Streak & Habit Building**:
   - Reward daily practice streaks to drive 30-day user retention.

---

## 🌟 Phase 4: Scaling & Global Expansion (Day 31+)

1. **AI Voice-Over Audio Integration**:
   - Record and integrate guided voice-overs using `VOICE_OVER_SCRIPTS.md`.

2. **Localization**:
   - Translate App Store text into Japanese, Korean, German, and Spanish.
