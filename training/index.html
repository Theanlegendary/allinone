⚠️ On Hold — Reasons & Feedback
Why bills are Not Assigned or stuck in Delivery · How to provide feedback
📋 Common On-Hold Reasons
1
No rider assigned — Bill arrived at branch but no driver allocated yet
2
Customer not reachable — Phone off / no answer after 2+ attempts
3
Wrong / incomplete address — Cannot locate destination
4
COD dispute — Customer refuses payment amount
5
Parcel damaged / missing — Needs supervisor review before delivery
🎯 Required Actions
✅ Update status in system within 1 hour of identifying issue
✅ Call customer minimum 3 times before escalating
✅ Record reason with correct status code
✅ Escalate to supervisor if unresolved after 24 hours
⚠️ Bills >3 days old = urgent escalation required

🎯 KPI System — How It Works
Understanding the 10H KPI · Green vs Red targets · Hit Rate
📏 KPI Rules
🟢
Green KPI
≤ 10 Hours
Delivered within target
🔴
Red / Over KPI
> 10 Hours
Exceeds target — review required
✓
KPI clock starts when bill arrives at post office (status 306)
✓
Clock stops when bill is delivered (status 410)
!
Hit Rate = Green KPI bills ÷ Total completed × 100%
✗
VIP bills must achieve >95% hit rate — priority handling

📊 How to Read the Report
📌 Completed (410) — Total delivered bills today
🟢 Pending Green — Not yet delivered but still within 10H
🔴 Pending Red Over — Over 10H, needs urgent action
📈 Hit Rate % — Overall KPI performance percentage
📅 Today / Yesterday / This Week / Month — Trend columns
⭐ VIP — Special customers, must be prioritized
⏱️ >1 Day — Bills pending more than 24 hours
🔥 >3 Days — Critical overdue — immediate escalation

🌙 End of Day — Service Point Checklist
Tasks every service point must complete before close · Update inventory

1
Count all remaining parcels — Physical count must match system count
2
Update Not Assign bills — All unassigned must have a reason + next action
3
Scan all new arrivals — Incoming parcels scanned and in system before close
4
Send MEGA returns — Any return parcels dispatched to hub by 5PM
5
Escalate overdue bills — Report all >3-day bills to supervisor with reason
6
Submit daily report — Send EOD summary to group chat by 6PM
