02. On-Hold & Exception Management

Standardized escalation procedures for unassigned parcels and delivery delays.

Exception Protocol Guidelines
1

Unassigned Parcel Allocation (Status 306)

Parcels at service point must be assigned to driver routes within 2 hours of arrival.

2

Unreachable Receiver (Status 404)

Rider must make minimum 3 attempts spaced 30 minutes apart before logging exception.

3

Address Verification

If address is incomplete, verify with customer and update system notes before re-routing.
