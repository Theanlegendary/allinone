## 🌸 Session 1: Gentle Relief & Comfort (15 Minutes)

**Background Soundscape**: *Soft Wind Chimes & Light Rain* (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/remvze/moodist/main/public/sounds/things/wind-chimes.mp3`)

### Voice Tone:
*Ultra-soft, slow, warm, gentle, reassuring, whispered pacing with 4-second pauses between sentences.*

---

### 📜 Voice Script:

```text
[0:00 - 0:30]
(Soft rain playing gently in background)
Welcome to your peaceful sanctuary... Take a slow, gentle breath in... and let it softly fall away...
You are safe here... There is nowhere else you need to be... and nothing else you need to do right now...

[0:30 - 1:30]
Feel the gentle weight of your body resting... Allow your shoulders to drop half an inch...
Release any tightness in your forehead... your eyes... and your jaw...
With every soft exhale, let go of one small thought... Let it float away like leaves on a quiet river...

[1:30 - 3:00]
Imagine a warm, gentle light surrounding you... It is soft, comforting, and peaceful...
As you breathe in, invite this gentle warmth into your chest...
As you breathe out, release any heaviness, any worry, any fear...
You are doing wonderfully... You are whole... and you are safe...

[3:00 - 5:00]
Listen to the soft chime of the wind... Each tone is a gentle reminder that you are supported...
Let your breath return to its natural, effortless rhythm... Soft... easy... and calm...
Rest here in this gentle space as long as you like...
```
